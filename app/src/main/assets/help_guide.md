# TMC Load Tracker - Driver's Guide

Welcome to your professional hauling companion. This app is designed to help you track every mile
and every dollar with precision, specifically tailored for TMC operations.

---

## 🛰️ Real-Time GPS Tracking

The app uses high-accuracy GPS to measure your actual route.

* **Automatic Odometer:** Once you start a load, the app tracks your distance in the background.
* **Segment Switching:** The app tracks **Bounce** (Deadhead) and **Loaded** miles separately.
* **Notification Bar HUD:** You can see your live mileage and trip status by pulling down your
  phone's notification shade. You don't even need to keep the app open.
* **Auto-Resume:** If your phone restarts or the app is closed, tracking will automatically resume
  if a trip is still active.

---

## 💰 Payroll & Math Engine

The app calculates your pay using TMC's specific rules:

* **Payroll Week:** All totals are calculated based on the **Friday-to-Friday** pay cycle.
* **Deadhead Bonus:** If your dispatched deadhead is **150 miles or more**, the app automatically
  adds the **$0.20/mile** bonus to your net estimate.
* **Tarping Pay:** supports Steel ($30) and Lumber ($50) rates.
* **Pre-Tarped Logic:** Use the "Pre-Tarped" toggle to automatically halve the tarp bonus per
  company policy.
* **Training Mode:** Enable "Active Training Week" in the sidebar to add your flat-rate trainer
  premium to the weekly total.

---

## 🛣️ Mileage & OOR Protection

* **Out-of-Route (OOR) Audit:** The app compares your **Actual GPS Miles** against your **Dispatched
  Miles** to calculate your routing efficiency.
* **Going Home Protection:** Mark a load as "Going Home" to drive extra miles to your house without
  penalizing your weekly OOR percentage.

---

## 📝 Managing Your Trips

* **Active Editing:** Tap the Active Trip card on the Dashboard to **Edit** trip details if dispatch
  changes your orders, or **Cancel** the trip entirely.
* **Trip Notes:** Use the Notes field to record shipper delays, tarping issues, or specific site
  directions.
* **Timestamps:** The app automatically records the exact second of pickup and delivery for your
  records.

---

## 📤 Data & Privacy

* **CSV Export:** Open the sidebar and tap **"Export Payroll to CSV"** to generate a spreadsheet of
  your entire history. You can email this directly to yourself or payroll.
* **Night Vision Mode:** The app features a **Pure Black (#000000)** Dark Mode designed to preserve
  your night vision and save battery during long night hauls.
* **Data Safety:** Your records are stored in a hardened local database. App updates are designed to
  migrate your data safely without ever wiping your history.

---

## 🛠️ Quick Support

* **PRO # Locked?** Once a trip starts, the PRO Number is locked to protect database integrity. If
  you entered the wrong PRO #, simply cancel the trip and start a new one.
* **Mileage Not Updating?** Ensure your phone's Location permission is set to "Allow all the time"
  and battery optimization is disabled for this app.
