# Load Tracker Pro — Future Upgrades & Roadmap (fu.txt)

## 💰 3-Tier Freemium Monetization & Upgrade Architecture

### Tier 1: Free Tier ($0.00 / Free Download)
- **Target Audience**: Trial drivers, company drivers testing the app.
- **Features**:
  - Core PRO number load logging.
  - Real-time GPS Odometer & Bounce / Loaded tracking.
  - Friday-to-Thursday TMC payroll estimates (27% / 31% rates, tarp pay, trainer pay, deadhead bonus).
  - CSV Payload History Export.

### Tier 2: Pro Driver Tier ($9.99 One-Time / In-App Purchase)
- **Target Audience**: Professional W2 Company Drivers.
- **SKU ID**: `load_tracker_pro_driver` ($9.99)
- **Features Unlocked**:
  - 🗺️ **Individual Trip Route Maps & Global Route Heatmaps**.
  - 🏢 **Facility Insights Search**: Historical gate codes, parking notes, and entrance instructions indexed by `[Business Name] — [City, State]`.
  - ✏️ **Historical Record Editing**: Ability to edit completed loads to correct gross pay, tarping selections, and trip notes.

### Tier 3: Owner-Operator Command Center ($19.99 One-Time / $10.00 Discounted Upgrade)
- **Target Audience**: Independent Contractors, Lease Operators, 1099 Drivers.
- **SKU IDs**:
  - `load_tracker_owner_operator` ($19.99 direct unlock)
  - `load_tracker_pro_to_owner_op_upgrade` ($10.00 discounted upgrade for drivers who previously bought Pro Driver)
- **Features Unlocked**:
  - 📊 **Live Profit & Loss (P&L) Dashboard**: Net Operating Profit = Gross Load Pay - (Fuel + Truck Expenses + Tolls + Fixed Costs).
  - 📈 **Cost-Per-Mile (CPM) & Margin Analytics**: Live Cost-Per-Mile vs Revenue-Per-Mile calculation.
  - ⛽ **1-Tap Fuel & Maintenance Logger**: Diesel fill-ups, gallons, station discounts, and truck maintenance receipts.
  - 📑 **IFTA State Tax Mileage Summaries**: Automatic quarterly fuel tax summaries by state.

## 🚀 v2.0.0 Owner-Operator Architecture Roadmap
